Privacy Policy — TomVideoDownloader

Last updated: 2026-06-22

TomVideoDownloader (“the Extension”, “we”, “our”) is a browser extension that detects videos on web pages you choose to visit and lets you download them to your own computer. This policy explains what the Extension does and does not do with your information.

The Extension does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. Everything it does happens locally, inside your own browser.

1. What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect personal information (name, email, address, phone number, etc.).
  • We do not run analytics, telemetry, tracking pixels, or fingerprinting.
  • We do not operate servers that receive your browsing history, the pages you visit, the videos you detect, or the videos you download.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not require an account, sign-in, or registration.

2. What information the Extension handles, and why

All items below are processed locally on your device and are never sent to us.

The Extension requests no permission that is not used by a feature described above.

4. Host access (mainstream build)

The Chrome Web Store build declares host access only for mainstream video and social platforms (for example Vimeo, Dailymotion, Twitch, Bilibili, TED, Reddit, Twitter/X, TikTok, and similar public video sites). The detection script runs only on pages that match those declared patterns — not on arbitrary sites.

A separate full build distributed outside the Chrome Web Store may offer broader site support under different manifest permissions. That build is not described by this policy URL.

5. Data storage and retention

  • Settings, queue entries, and Library items remain on your device until you clear them (via in-extension controls, by uninstalling, or by clearing browser data).
  • The Extension keeps no server-side copy because it operates no backend.

6. Downloaded files

Files you download are saved by your browser to a location you choose. The Extension does not upload, copy, or transmit them elsewhere.

7. Third parties

The Extension communicates with the website you are viewing only to detect and fetch media you asked for — the same connections your browser would make to play that media. There is no third-party analytics SDK, ad network, or tracking provider embedded in the Extension.

8. Children’s privacy

The Extension is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Your responsibility for content

The Extension is a neutral tool for downloading media you have the right to access. You are responsible for complying with each site’s terms of service and with applicable copyright and local law.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date at the top will change. Material changes will be reflected in the Extension’s store listing.
